About the Department of Electronic Media

Established by Bangalore University to meet the surge in demand for professional, technologically proficient media personnel across television channels, radio stations, and emerging digital networks.

Origins & Evolution

Growth in television channels, radio stations, and web-based media platforms has created a strong demand for skilled manpower to produce electronic media content by utilizing current industry technologies. To cater to this demand, Bangalore University took the initiative and established the Department of Electronic Media, launching the two-year M.Sc. in Electronic Media post-graduate program in the 2002–03 academic year.

Though the curriculum maintains a pragmatic balance of theory and practice, paramount emphasis is placed on hands-on practical training. Students are comprehensively prepared for careers in television news and entertainment networks, radio broadcasting, digital production companies, and academia.

In the 2019–20 academic year, recognizing the rapid expansion of entertainment and animation sectors, Bangalore University introduced two specialized master's degree courses: M.Sc. in Graphics & Animation and M.Sc. in Film Making alongside the established M.Sc. in Electronic Media.

OUR VISION

Vision Statement

"The Department of Electronic Media will impart media education, research, and training on par with international standards with advanced technology to construct the information-driven society. It will contribute ethically sensitive and socially responsible and empowered human resource to the field of mass media."
OUR MISSION

Mission Statement

  • 1 Industry Readiness: Train students to meet the requirements of electronic media organizations with special focus on television, cinema, radio, and new media.
  • 2 Interdisciplinary Competence: Educate students in the areas of advertising, corporate communication, media management, and media research.
  • 3 Social Accountability: Educate students to be socially responsible, culturally sensitive, and ethically and legally correct.

Curriculum & Admissions

Academic Programs Offered

Post-Graduate Master of Science (M.Sc.) and Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) degree programs conducted under the faculty of Science at Bangalore University.

M.Sc. in Electronic Media

Two-year regular post-graduate course established in the 2002–03 academic year to build comprehensive broadcast production capability.

Duration 2 Years (4 Semesters, Regular)
Total Intake 35 Regular + 5 Payment Seats (40 Total)
Eligibility Criteria Any Bachelor's Degree with 50% Marks (5% relaxation for SC/ST)

Curriculum & Training Focus

Television production and planning, cinematography, sound and lighting, video editing, radio program production, visual communication, presentation techniques for radio and TV, web journalism, news gathering and writing for electronic media, new media technology, media management, advertising, corporate communication, media ethics, and media research.

Television Production Cinematography & Lighting Radio Production Audio Recording & Editing Non-Linear Video Editing Web Journalism Media Research Corporate Communication
Mandatory Practical Capstones:
  • 20-Minute Documentary: Every student produces and directs an independent broadcast-standard documentary.
  • BU News Bulletin: Production and webcasting of a daily 30-minute news magazine.
  • Professional Internship: Eight-week to four-month full-time internship with reputed media houses, TV news channels, or radio stations.

Faculty members have undertaken Minor Research Projects and actively participate, present research papers in national and international conferences, and publish papers in reputed peer-reviewed journals.

Centralized Online Admission System

Admission to M.Sc. courses is made strictly in order of merit through the centralized online admission system of Bangalore University. Admissions occur once a year via public notification published by the University Registrar. Candidates holding any Bachelor's degree with 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST) are eligible to apply.
